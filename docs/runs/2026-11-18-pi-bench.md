# Pi 3B+ bench run, 2026-11-18

## What ran

The owner ran Part A, steps 1 to 7, of
[`sheets/bench.md`](sheets/bench.md) at commit `39c5fa4`. The host was a
Raspberry Pi 3 Model B+ (`wmr-bridge`, Debian 13). The phone was a Pixel 11 Pro
with `com.onyxlens.wmr_companion`. Both WMR controllers stayed on their existing
bonds: LEFT `2C:4D:79:C8:C9:CC`, RIGHT `2C:4D:79:C8:F9:A9`. The session ran
21:30 to 23:00 EST. Part B, steps 8 to 12, is parked by the owner (see below).

The versions changed during the session. The Pi started on `0.3.28-rc.1` and the
app on `0.3.25`. Both were upgraded mid-run. The Pi went to `0.3.28-rc.2` from the
release `.deb` with the checksum checked against `SHA256SUMS`. The app went to
`0.3.26` with `adb install -r` of the release APK. The sheet names app `v0.3.25`,
which predates `ONY-1476`. The failover that steps 4 and 5 test first ships in
`v0.3.26`. Each result below names the version pair that was live.

These figures are user-reported. The owner ran the hardware and kept the record.
This page restates that record. The journal and probe output stayed on the Pi and
are not in this repository.

### Step 2 range

The owner walked a room with both controllers and ended about 20 ft from the Pi,
which sat behind a chair. The sheet gives a loss limit of 3.0 % per hand and no
range, line-of-sight or interference condition. Record this result as a failure
at about 20 ft behind furniture. A retest at nominal range is still owed before
anyone calls it a product defect.

## Anomalies

- The adapter crashed on its own at 22:23:52, with no service restart involved.
  The log shows `hci0: hardware error 0x00`, then an HCI Reset timeout, then
  `Powered=no`. The `wmr-bridge-bt-recover` unit failed again because `hciuart` is
  `not-found` on this image. Both controllers were awake the whole time. They
  read `Paired: yes Trusted: yes Connected: no` only because the adapter was off.
- WMR controllers have no sleep stage. They are on, idle or off (owner,
  2026-11-18). Motion does not wake them from a sleep state, because no such
  state exists.
- LEFT dropped its hidraw link at 22:13:05 after 131,689 reports and reconnected
  3 s later. No block, power-off or cooldown line came with it. The owner
  confirms that nobody touched either controller at that moment, so this is a
  spontaneous hidraw or ACL drop, not the idle policy.
- A restart of `wmr-bridge` at 21:47:49 left `hci0` at `Powered=no` with
  `Opcode 0x0c03 failed: -110` until a reboot.
- The probe harness prints `passed: false` with `records: 0` when no controller
  data arrives. That reads as a gate failure instead of "no data".

## Open items

- Step 2 at nominal range: retest owed. The RIGHT figure is the only gate failure
  with an open range question.
- Step 4: needs app-side instrumentation, or a read of the app Debug screen
  during an outage. The bridge cannot time a takeover it cannot see.
- Step 5: tracked as `wmr-companion#337`.
- Step 7: needs the 30/90 s override, a service restart, and a fix for the
  adapter crash first.
- Decode CPU: tracked as `WMR_Bridge#262`.
- Sheet drift found during this run is in `ONY-1509`. That covers the wrong
  advertised name in step 1, the app version in the header, the range-free loss
  limit in step 2, and the `records: 0` message.

## Method

The session ran from `liesa` over `ssh wmrpi`, with `adb` for the phone. The
health check before each step was `/home/liesa/bin/wmr-pi-check.sh`. The bench
verbs on the Pi were `/usr/local/sbin/wmr-agent {status,restart,link,wlan-cycle,probe}`.
